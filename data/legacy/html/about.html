Most creator economy advice is optimized for selling hope. Mine is optimized for what actually works.

Distinguished gentleman on a left is me in 1999.

Getting my hands on a computer early on really lit the spark for my digital creativity. I remember diving into designing websites for any game I have been playing online.

That was the beginning of my love affair with web and interface design.

When I was fifteen, I started reaching out to companies to design their websites. I had this policy – they only had to pay if they loved what I created.

This approach really set the stage for my freelance career. I've done all sorts of projects, from designing a therapeutic app in Africa to uber-like app for strippers in Australia.

My policy paid of in testimonials from my Upwork's clients.

But it wasn't all about professional work.

I've been part of all kinds of projects – dabbling in stock photography and illustrations, marketing and production for online courses, writing articles and creating a videos.

I've always been up for new challenges and learning as I go.
