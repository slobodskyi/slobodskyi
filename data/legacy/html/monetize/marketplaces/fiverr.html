What is Fiverr?

Fiverr is a marketplace for freelancers that allows designers, programmers, copywriters, and many others to sell their services.

Services are sold as "gigs," which include a description, delivery time, and price.

Here's an example of gigs in the logo creation category.

How to Earn on Fiverr?

Step by Step:

  1. Sign Up: Set up and design your profile.
  2. Create Gigs: Describe your services, prices, and deadlines, and add high-quality visuals.
  3. Use Keywords: This will help with visibility in searches.
  4. Communicate Quickly and Professionally with Clients: This will set you apart from the competition.
  5. Deliver Quality Work: To garner positive reviews and attract new clients.

What Services Can You Offer?

Fiverr hosts a plethora of categories, from creative to technical and marketing services.

Services with clear and specific titles tend to perform the best.

For instance:

  1. I will create awesome children's book illustrations.
  2. I will set up and optimize your social media business pages.
  3. I will write original and impressive SEO articles.
  4. I will create infinite loop animation GIFs.
  5. I will record a deep male voice-over.

Check out popular gigs on Fiverr to get an idea of what category you can offer your services in.

How Much Can You Earn on Fiverr?

Earnings depend on the type of service, skill level, and demand.

Some might earn a few dollars per project, while specialists in certain fields can make hundreds or thousands of dollars per project.

Your income will be based on the number and quality of jobs completed, your ability to promote your services, and your relationship-building skills with clients.

Originally, Fiverr's concept was that services were affordable, starting at $5 (hence the name Fiverr).

However, during the ordering process, you can choose additional options, and the final cost may be higher.

Example:

  1. An illustrative-style portrait costs $20 per person.
  2. For two people, it would be $40.
  3. Rushing the order can increase the price to $100.
  4. If high-resolution files are needed, that's an additional $50.

How to Create an Account on Fiverr?

To create an account, go to Fiverr and sign up.

To create a gig and offer your service to potential clients, click on the "Become a Seller" button.

How to Create a Gig on Fiverr?

To create a gig, navigate to Gigs → Create a new Gig.

The texts must be written in English.

To write and translate, learn how to use ChatGPT — a free tool powered by artificial intelligence.

  1. Fill in the title: Provide a brief and clear title that reflects the essence of the service you're offering.
  2. Choose a category and subcategory: Determine which category and subcategory your service belongs to, making it easier for users to find.
  3. Create a description: In the description, detail what your service includes, the terms, and the benefits for the client. Be clear and specific so potential clients understand what they will receive.
  4. Set your price and deadlines: Establish a price for your service and expected turnaround times. You can offer different service packages at various prices.
  5. Add tags: Use keywords to improve your visibility in Fiverr's search.
  6. Upload images or videos: Add high-quality images or videos that visually demonstrate your service and examples of completed work.
  7. Publish your gig: Once you've filled in all the necessary fields and reviewed your information, publish your gig.

There are hints and video instructions to help you fill everything out.

Let's briefly walk through each step.

Overview

Come up with a title for your gig in the "I will..." format.

If you're creating illustrations, your gig would be titled "I will create illustrations + your details".

  1. Use a specific title: The title should clearly reflect the service offered, be concise and understandable.
  2. Create a detailed description: In the gig description, it's crucial to explain in detail what your service includes, the benefits it offers to the client, and the workflow process.

Pricing

Specify the cost and details of your gig.

Depending on the chosen category, this section may vary slightly.

In my example, I chose the SEO category, and the services loaded from this category.

  1. Set a reasonable price: Set competitive prices that match the quality and scope of the services offered.
  2. Use different packages: Consider offering several service packages at different price levels.

Description

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) plays a key role in improving the visibility of your gig on Fiverr:

  1. Use keywords: Incorporate target keywords in the title, description, and tags of your gig to improve its search rankings on Fiverr.
  2. Optimize the description: Use keywords naturally in the description, making the text easy to read and informative.
  3. Add tags: Choose relevant tags that accurately describe your service and match the queries of potential clients.

An effective gig description convinces potential clients that your service is exactly what they need:

  1. Highlight benefits: Focus on how your service can help the client, not just on listing features.
  2. Structure the text: Break the description into paragraphs, use lists, and subheadings for better readability.

Requirements

If you need additional information from the client to perform your service, specify what exactly is needed at this step.

For example, for SEO services, I would need a link to the website.

Gallery

Images and videos can significantly enhance the appeal of your gig:

  1. Use High-Quality Files: High-resolution photos and videos make your offer appear more professional and reliable in the eyes of clients.
  2. Showcase Your Work: Use images and videos to visually demonstrate your skills and examples of completed projects.
  3. Upload a Video and Talk About Your Service: A video greeting can establish a personal connection with potential clients and make your gig stand out among competitors.

Publish

At this step, you'll be asked if you are a US tax resident.

Indicate that you are not a US tax resident if you are not American, do not have a business, or legal entities in the US.

After this, your offer will become available on Fiverr, and you will be able to receive orders.

How to Promote Your Profile?

For effective promotion:

  1. Analyze Competitors: Study the offers of your competitors to understand market trends and identify new niches.
  2. Provide Quality Service: Aim to exceed the expectations of your first clients to receive positive reviews, which will help increase the visibility of your profile.
  3. Diversify: Depending on your services, other platforms may also be suitable for work. For example, if you're an illustrator, explore how to sell on Shutterstock and how to set up a shop on Etsy.

How to Withdraw Money from Fiverr?

Follow these steps:

  1. Go to the Earnings Section: Log into your Fiverr account and navigate to the "Earnings" section, which displays your available balance.
  2. Choose a Withdrawal Method: Fiverr offers several withdrawal methods, including Payoneer, PayPal, and bank transfer. Choose the most convenient method for you.
  3. Minimum Withdrawal Amount: Note that there might be a minimum withdrawal amount set for each payment system, usually around $30.

How to Withdraw Money from Fiverr to Payoneer?

Payoneer is a popular payment system for withdrawing money from freelance marketplaces and receiving payments from clients.

  1. Create an account, receive a Payoneer bank card, and use it at any ATM or for online payments.
  2. You can also transfer money to a local bank account with a 2% fee.

I have an article that goes into detail about the fees and the process of withdrawing money through Payoneer.

How to Withdraw Money from Fiverr to PayPal?

  1. If you are in a country where PayPal operates and are comfortable with the fees (4% - 7%), select the "PayPal Account" option.
  2. In Ukraine, PayPal does not support money withdrawal. You can spend funds from your account, but you cannot transfer them to a Ukrainian bank account.

Do you need to pay taxes on income from Fiverr?

If you're just starting out and unsure about the success of your venture, you might not have to worry about it immediately.

However, if your earnings begin to pick up, you could end up having to pay taxes retroactively.

Once your income becomes steady, it's advisable to register as an individual entrepreneur (FOP) and start paying taxes.

Reviews about Fiverr

Positive aspects:

  1. A Variety of Services: Fiverr offers a multitude of services, from design to marketing.
  2. Ease of Use: The platform is convenient for both sellers and buyers.
  3. Opportunities for Freelancers: An excellent platform for both beginners and professionals.
  4. Flexibility: Freelancers can choose projects and set their prices.

Negative aspects:

  1. High Competition: It can be hard to stand out in popular categories.
  2. Fees: Fiverr takes a 20% commission from each transaction, which some freelancers find high.
  3. Restrictions for Sellers: There are limitations on communicating with clients outside the platform.

Frequently Asked Questions about Fiverr

How can I optimize my Fiverr gig for on-platform SEO?

Start by researching keywords relevant to your niche and incorporate them naturally in your gig title, description, and tags. Use a clear, structured layout that highlights your service benefits. Regularly update your content based on performance analytics to ensure it stays competitive in Fiverr's search rankings.

What strategies can I use to earn more client reviews?

Focus on delivering high-quality work and meeting deadlines consistently. After completing a project, follow up with a polite message asking for feedback, and consider offering a small bonus for detailed reviews. Positive reviews boost your profile’s credibility and help attract more clients.

How do I communicate effectively with potential Fiverr clients?

Respond promptly and professionally to client inquiries. Ask clarifying questions to fully understand their requirements and confirm details before starting a project. Clear and consistent communication helps build trust and sets the stage for successful collaboration.

What common mistakes should I avoid when creating a Fiverr gig?

Ensure you avoid common pitfalls such as vague service descriptions, underpricing, and using low-quality visuals. Clearly outline what’s included in your service, invest in high-resolution images or videos, and set realistic delivery times. This attention to detail helps prevent misunderstandings and builds client confidence.

How do I determine competitive pricing for my services on Fiverr?

Research similar gigs in your niche to gauge market rates. Factor in your experience, the time required for each project, and any additional costs. Experiment with tiered pricing or packages to offer more value at different price points, ensuring you remain competitive while also fairly compensating your expertise.

What additional services can I offer to boost my Fiverr income?

Think about offering gig extras such as expedited delivery, extra revisions, or bundled services. These add-ons not only provide more value to your clients but also increase your overall earnings per order. Consider surveying clients to understand what additional services they might be interested in.

How can I leverage Fiverr analytics to improve my gig performance?

Regularly review your gig’s performance metrics, such as views, clicks, and conversion rates, available in Fiverr’s analytics dashboard. Use this data to refine your keywords, adjust pricing, or update your gig content. Tracking performance over time helps you understand what resonates with potential clients and identify areas for improvement.

How do I handle a difficult client or negative feedback on Fiverr?

When faced with a difficult client or negative feedback, maintain a professional and calm demeanor. Address the issue directly by understanding the client’s concerns and offering solutions or clarifications. Use constructive criticism as a learning opportunity to improve your services, and if necessary, reach out to Fiverr support for guidance on resolving disputes.

What are the best practices for creating a professional Fiverr profile?

Create a standout profile by using a high-quality, professional photo and writing a clear bio that highlights your skills and experience. Include a portfolio that showcases your best work, and ensure your profile is consistently updated. A well-crafted profile builds credibility and makes a strong first impression on potential clients.

How can I expand my freelancing business beyond Fiverr?

Leverage your success on Fiverr by promoting your services on social media, creating a professional website, and exploring additional freelance platforms. Diversifying your presence can lead to more opportunities, higher income, and a broader client base. Cross-promote your gigs and share success stories to build a personal brand that extends beyond Fiverr.
