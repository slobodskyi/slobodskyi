How to secure your account?

  1. Use unique passwords: Create a unique password for your OnlyFans account, for example, through LastPass. The same passwords increase the risk of hacking. Check on haveibeenpwned.com to see if your old passwords have been leaked.
  2. Enable two-step authentication: Use SMS or the Google Authenticator app for additional protection when logging in from a new device. You can do this in "Settings → Account → Two Step Authentication."
  3. Protect your SIM card: Make sure that your SIM card cannot be reissued without your knowledge. Check the security settings on your operator’s website and avoid mentioning your number online unnecessarily.

How to protect your identity on Onlyfans?

  1. Do not disclose your address: If someone wants to send a gift, use parcel lockers or general delivery postal offices.
  2. Be cautious with your phone number: If you need to share your number, use a separate SIM card. Note that OnlyFans rules prohibit moving communication to other platforms, but sometimes subscribers share their contacts, and if you decide to write to them, do not use your primary number.
  3. Control personal information in photos and videos: Make sure your documents, recognizable interior details, or street views are not in the frame.
  4. Block unwanted users: Do not hesitate to block those who make you uncomfortable or violate your boundaries.
  5. Restrict access to your page from certain countries: You can choose which country’s users will not have access to your page. Go to "Settings → Privacy and Safety → Block by country." Note that users can bypass this restriction using VPNs.
