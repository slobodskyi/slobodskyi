How to Withdraw Money from Fansly?

Let's figure out how to get the money.

Payout Methods

The minimum withdrawal amount depends on the payout method:

  1. Payment systems like Paxum and Cosmopayment: $20.
  2. SEPA transfer (Bank Deposit within the EU and UK): $50.
  3. Bank Wire (International SWIFT transfer): $100.
  4. Cryptocurrency: $100.

Payout Time

After a user spends money, it will be in "Pending balance" status for 7 days.

Then, the money will be available for payout.

You can request a payout by selecting the amount you want to withdraw and the payment system.

After the request, the money will arrive within 3-5 days.
