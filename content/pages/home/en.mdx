## What I'm Building

Everything I write here gets tested on real projects first.

1. [UNITED24 Media](https://united24media.com/) — Ukraine's media outlet for international audiences. I build **internal AI tools for the team**, monitoring systems for the newsroom, and keep the site visible in both Google and AI search.
2. [Zori.bio](https://zori.bio/) — my **link-in-bio tool for content creators**. I've reviewed enough of these platforms to know what they get wrong, so I built my own.
3. [Wabi.school](https://wabi.school/en) — an **LMS for creators** who want to research, create, and sell online courses. Human first, AI second. In closed beta.
4. **Course launches with creators** — I partner with experts to turn what they know into online courses: illustrator [Anna Lomakina](https://www.instagram.com/lomakina.space), yoga teacher [Yana Borodina](https://www.instagram.com/scorpioprincesse/), barber [Gena Maguza](https://www.instagram.com/maguza/). They bring the craft. I bring the systems.
5. **Custom systems for small businesses** — a PMS + CRM for [SeaSky apart-hotel in Odesa](https://sea-sky.od.ua/), and an internal management system for «[Хто це?](https://www.htotse.com/)», a Ukrainian book publisher. Boring software that saves real hours.

If I recommend a tool on this site, chances are I've already run it in production.
