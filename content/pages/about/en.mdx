I've spent over a decade in the creator economy — not selling dreams, but actually testing platforms, building systems, and helping real creators earn money.

Since 2014, my guides have reached 2M+ creators. I've helped grow a YouTube channel from 0 to 350,000 subscribers, set up international payments for 300+ creators, and consulted on adult creator platforms generating $10K+/month.

Based in Kyiv, Ukraine. Originally from Odesa.

Most creator economy advice is optimized for selling hope. Mine is optimized for what actually works.

## What I Actually Do

I test monetization platforms so you don't waste time on the wrong ones.

My process: sign up, use the platform myself or with clients, document what works and what doesn't, then write about it honestly — including the parts that suck.

Platforms I've worked with directly:

- [Fansly](/monetize/memberships/fansly) — 3+ years consulting, helped creators reach $10K+/month
- [OnlyFans](/monetize/memberships/onlyfans) — Direct consulting experience with adult creators
- [Payoneer](/monetize/payments/payoneer) — Helped 2,000+ creators set up international payments
- [Patreon](/monetize/memberships/patreon), [Gumroad](/monetize/marketplaces/gumroad), [Stripe](/monetize/payments/stripe) — Personal use + client implementations

I also cover payment systems ([PayPal](/monetize/payments/paypal), [Wise](/monetize/payments/wise), [Skrill](/monetize/payments/skrill)), marketplaces ([Fiverr](/monetize/marketplaces/fiverr), [Etsy](/monetize/marketplaces/etsy)), and [AI tools for creators](/create/ai/chatgpt).

## Who I Help (And Who I Don't)

**This site is for:**

- Creators who want sustainable income, not viral fame
- International creators dealing with payment headaches
- People who prefer boring systems over motivational fluff
- Anyone tired of "guru" advice that doesn't work in practice

**This is NOT for:**

- People looking for get-rich-quick schemes
- Those who want someone to blame instead of solutions
- Anyone expecting magic formulas or secret hacks

For over a decade, I've deeply engaged in helping individuals in the creative economy discover ways to earn a living and [develop their monetization strategies](/monetize).

Getting my hands on a computer early on really lit the spark for my digital creativity. I remember diving into designing websites for any game I have been playing online.

That was the beginning of my love affair with web and interface design.

When I was fifteen, I started reaching out to companies to design their websites. I had this policy – they only had to pay if they loved what I created.

This approach really set the stage for my freelance career. I've done all sorts of projects, from designing a therapeutic app in Africa to uber-like app for strippers in Australia.

My policy paid of in testimonials from my [Upwork's clients](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/%7E018693d655dc8e8958).
