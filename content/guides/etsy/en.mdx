## What is Etsy?

[Etsy](https://slobo.click/etsy) is a marketplace where a variety of items are sold, including:

1. **Handmade Crafts:** Original creations made from various materials.
2. **Vintage:** Items over 20 years old, ranging from clothing to furniture.
3. **Craft Supplies:** Everything needed for crafting and art.
4. **Digital Products:** Templates, illustrations, and e-books.
5. **Customized Goods:** Made-to-order items, from portraits to jewelry.

Etsy Payments is Etsy's payment solution for convenient transactions. Check the list of countries [where Etsy Payments is available](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015710408-Countries-Eligible-for-Etsy-Payments?segment=selling).

To withdraw your earnings, you will need to create an account with the [payment system Payoneer](https://slobo.click/payoneer).

I have a separate article on [how Payoneer works](/monetize/payments/payoneer).

### How to start on Etsy?

Steps to follow:

1. **Registration and opening a shop:** Choose a shop name, add shipping and return policies.
2. **Creating listings:** Add your products with detailed descriptions, prices, and high-quality photos.
3. **Payment setup:** Enable Etsy Payments to receive money from buyers.
4. **Shop promotion:** Use social media, blogs, and other channels to promote your products and shop.
5. **Order management:** Keep track of orders, ship products on time, and provide excellent customer service.

### What are Etsy's fees?

1. **Listing fee:** $0.20 per item.
2. **Transaction fee:** 5% of the item price.
3. **Payment processing fee:** 2.9% + $0.25 per transaction.

## How to promote your shop on Etsy?

To effectively promote your Etsy shop, use these strategies:

1. **SEO optimization:** Use relevant keywords in titles and descriptions to improve visibility on Etsy and search engines. More details about [how to get traffic from search engines](/connect/organic/seo).
2. **High-quality photos:** Take clear, well-lit, attractive photos from various angles to attract potential buyers.
3. Social media marketing: Share your products on social media platforms like Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and Twitter. [Use hashtags](/connect/organic/hashtags), stories, and posts to engage more people.
4. **Customer reviews:** Encourage satisfied customers to leave positive reviews. High ratings and good reviews can boost your shop's trustworthiness and attract more buyers.
5. **Etsy ads:** Consider using Etsy's advertising platform to directly promote your listings.

## How to withdraw money from Etsy?

To be able to sell on Etsy, you'll need to enable Etsy Payments and create an account with the [payment system Payoneer](https://slobo.click/payoneer) when setting up your shop.

### What is Etsy Payment?

Etsy Payments is a convenient way for sellers to accept payments.

This system consolidates various payment methods, allowing purchases directly on the Etsy site.

Etsy offers the most convenient payment options in each country, helping to increase sales and profits.

You don't need to worry about payment processing, and you can use the saved time to develop new products.

<Shot src="./678799f09cff484104b990d2_etsy-payment.webp" alt="Payment options through Etsy Payments" wide />

To withdraw money from Etsy, you need to use Payoneer.

### What is Payoneer?

[Payoneer](https://slobo.click/payoneer) is a convenient way to receive payments for freelancers and sellers on marketplaces.

Registration with Payoneer is free.

You can transfer money in dollars to your local bank card or get a Payoneer bank card and not transfer funds to an account in your local bank.

I have an article that [explains this payment system in detail](/monetize/payments/payoneer).

How does withdrawal from Etsy and Payoneer work?

1. When you register your shop on Etsy, you link it to your Payoneer account.
2. Buyers pay for your items on the site through Etsy Payments, and all earned money is stored on Etsy.
3. Then, you can withdraw it from Etsy to Payoneer, and from Payoneer to your bank account.

## Do you need to pay taxes on income from Etsy?

If you're just starting out and aren't sure how successful your venture will be, you might not need to worry about it for now.

In the worst-case scenario, you may have to pay taxes retroactively.

However, once your sales become regular, it makes sense to [register as an individual entrepreneur (FOP) and start paying taxes](/monetize/payments/taxes).
