## What is Gumroad?

[Gumroad](https://slobo.click/gumroad) is a platform for selling digital products.

Artists, musicians, writers, developers, and other creators use Gumroad to quickly and easily [start monetizing their content](/monetize).

What types of digital products are sold on Gumroad?

1. E-books.
2. Courses and guides.
3. Icons and mockups.
4. Plugins and software.
5. Illustrations and brushes.
6. 3D models.

Plus many more.

In this article, we will discuss in detail how to create an account, add a product, make your first sales, and withdraw money.

### How does Gumroad work?

It's simple.

1. You need to create an account, add a product, and set a price.
2. After publishing the product, you'll receive a link that can be shared on your blog.
3. Gumroad handles payment processing, and the money, after deducting a fee, is sent to you.

Example of a product page on Gumroad - [Nakamoto](https://wzx.gumroad.com/l/Nakamoto?a=645637747).

This is a tutorial on creating a photorealistic CG portrait.

<Shot src="./66f40eecab0c833d44d4721d_65c4e6d3bd4cb1fa89e78073_what-is-gumroad.webp" alt="Page selling 3D models on Gumroad" wide />

Important!

**You can only earn money on Gumroad if you are in a country where the Stripe payment system or a PayPal business account operates.**

The list of countries will be in the section about [withdrawing money from Gumroad](/monetize/marketplaces/gumroad#how-to-withdraw-money-from-gumroad).

If you don't have access to Stripe or PayPal, you can look for an [alternative to Gumroad](/monetize/marketplaces/gumroad#alternatives-to-gumroad).

Some alternative services offer the same features with lower commissions.

### What is Gumroad's commission?

The commission is 10%.

You only pay a commission on sales.

There is no fee for creating or maintaining an account.

### How to create a product on Gumroad?

To add a new product, [create an account](https://slobo.click/gumroad) and go to Products → [New product](https://app.gumroad.com/products/new).

Creation happens in three stages.

1. Product: come up with a name, set a price.
2. Content: add content, upload files.
3. Share: get a link to the published product.

### Product

Choose the product type, come up with a name, and set a price.

What types of products are there?

1. Digital product.
2. Course or tutorial.
3. E-book.
4. Membership.
5. Physical good.
6. Bundle.

At the next step, write a description and upload a cover image.

The description typically provides detailed information about the product, including screenshots and videos of what the buyer will receive.

### Email campaigns

How do email campaigns work?

Go to Emails → New email.

1. Choose the recipients: everyone or those who bought a certain product.
2. Write the email: you can add images and links.
3. Send it: either immediately or schedule it for later.

After sending the email, you can see how many times it was opened or the links were clicked.

<Shot src="./66f40eebab0c833d44d47058_65c4df6fc94757f68574994c_new-email-or-post.webp" alt="Creating newsletters through Gumroad" wide />

How to use email campaigns?

A simple example from one of the accounts I helped develop:

1. We had a free product downloaded by 14,000 people.
2. The author was working on a paid course. We sent out an email campaign to talk about the upcoming course and gather questions on the course topic.
3. When the course was ready, we sent out an email with advertisements and a discount for the course purchase.

Out of 14,000 emails sent, about 3,000 opened the email, and more than 200 people bought the course.

Win-win.

The course turned out to be of higher quality because we took into account the feedback during its development. Plus, we achieved good sales through the advertising in the newsletter.

### Email campaign automation

On Gumroad, you can set up automatic emails to be sent to users who have taken a specific action.

This is set up in the → [Workflows section](https://app.gumroad.com/workflows).

What types of actions are there?

1. Purchase.
2. New subscriber.
3. Member cancels.
4. New affiliate.

We'll talk more about affiliates and the affiliate program later.

There are filters for each action.

For example, for a purchase, we can specify a particular product or set a minimum amount.

With Workflows, you can advertise your products, welcome new subscribers, or offer a discount to users who have canceled their subscription.

### Affiliate Program

With Gumroad's [affiliate program](/monetize/advertisement/affiliate-marketing), you can earn in two ways:

1. Allow other creators to advertise your products for a percentage of the sales.
2. Advertise other creators' products yourself.

How does the affiliate program Work?

1. In the → Affiliates section, select the products for which you want to enable the affiliate program and set the percentage your affiliate will receive.
2. Place the link to join the affiliate program where your audience can see it.
3. Once someone applies, you can approve the participant, and they will receive unique links to advertise your products.

Gumroad handles payment processing and the payout of commissions.

<Shot src="./66f40eebab0c833d44d4705e_65ca4c736509241598149d7b_gumroad-affliates.webp" alt="Gumroad affiliate program" wide />

#### How to earn from advertising other authors' products?

You can earn from advertising other authors' products in two scenarios:

1. If they have an affiliate program enabled.
2. If their products are listed in Discover.

#### How to check if an author has an affiliate Program?

Go to their Gumroad page and add "/affiliates" at the end.

Example:

I found an author in Discover whose products I want to promote.

Their page URL is wrongop.gumroad.com.

To check for an affiliate program, I need to visit wrongop.gumroad.com/affiliates.

If an application form opens up – great, submit an application and wait for its approval.

<Shot src="./66f40eebab0c833d44d47061_65ca5438c392f57ef706b7c6_gumroad-become-an-affiliate.webp" alt="Application form for participation in the affiliate program" wide />

If the page doesn't open, it means the author hasn't enabled the affiliate program.

What then?

Go to the → [Gumroad Affiliates](https://app.gumroad.com/products/affiliated?affiliates=true) section and paste the link to the specific product into the Affiliate Link Generator.

Click Generate link to receive your referral link.

If a purchase is made through your link, you'll receive 10% of the product's price.

<Shot src="./66f40eebab0c833d44d47064_65c4e134d04bbc5b1b4f6791_gumroad-affiliates.webp" alt="Earning through the Gumroad affiliate program" wide />

You can also promote not just a specific product but the entire Discover section.

Discover aggregates popular products from various authors.

### How to launch advertising in Recommendations?

In the Share section when adding a product, select Boost and specify the percentage you are willing to give for advertising.

A minimum of 30% is required; the higher the percentage, the more frequently your product will appear in recommendations.

Advertising in Discover can be a good source of additional sales, but it shouldn't be your only strategy.

1. Develop your blog and social networks. Promoting products to a loyal audience works best.
2. For verified and quality products, consider launching paid targeted ads through Facebook or Google.
3. Find authors you admire and analyze how they engage with their audience.

## How to withdraw money from Gumroad?

The minimum withdrawal amount is $10.

Funds become available 7 days after a sale and are automatically transferred to you the following Friday.

Gumroad offers three payment methods:

1. Bank transfer.
2. Stripe.
3. PayPal.

To receive funds via bank transfer, you'll need to provide documents proving residency in one of the supported countries.

<Shot src="./66f40eecab0c833d44d4717a_65c9e6f1c0458e2f784819fc_gumroad-withdrawn-to-bank-availability.webp" alt="List of countries where money can be withdrawn via bank transfer from Gumroad" wide />

What if your country isn't listed?

Use Stripe or PayPal.

For example, in Ukraine, you [cannot open a PayPal business](/monetize/payments/paypal) account or [create a Stripe account](/monetize/payments/stripe).

Without Stripe and PayPal, withdrawing your earnings won't be possible, so you may need to [find an alternative to Gumroad](/monetize/marketplaces/gumroad#alternatives-to-gumroad).

<Shot src="./66f40eecab0c833d44d4717d_65c4de0e5c0d25305b11a5a6_gumroad-payout-method-paypal-stripe.webp" alt="Setting up money withdrawal from Gumroad through PayPal or Stripe" wide />

How to check if PayPal and Stripe are available in your country?

1. Stripe: Review the list of countries [where Stripe operates](https://stripe.com/global).
2. PayPal: Check if there is a ["Business" tab available](https://paypal.com).

<Shot src="./678799f66e9c6be6d25fb049_65c340bd1205f2a33b3ed308_paypal-business-account-availability.webp" alt="Understanding whether a business PayPal account is functioningеc-аккаунт PayPal" wide />

Found your country?

Great.

Create an account with the payment system and link it in Gumroad under Settings → [Payments](https://app.gumroad.com/settings/payments).

Didn't find your country?

No worries, we'll discuss alternatives to Gumroad next.

### Can you withdraw from Gumroad to Payoneer or Wise?

The [official Gumroad withdrawal instructions](https://help.gumroad.com/article/13-getting-paid) state that they do not support withdrawals to Payoneer or Wise.

However, I managed to do it.

I added my European bank account details from Payoneer to Gumroad.

After that, Gumroad requested documents to prove that I reside in the country of the account.

I thought that would be the end of it, but the funds were successfully withdrawn from Gumroad.

## Alternatives to Gumroad

The main drawbacks that lead people to look for Gumroad alternatives include high fees and the inability to withdraw funds.

We will look at three services:

1. [Buy me a coffee](https://slobo.click/buymeacoffee): a simple service for collecting donations and selling digital products.
2. [Patreon](https://slobo.click/patreon): earning through subscriptions and monetizing your community.
3. [Etsy](https://slobo.click/etsy): selling digital and physical goods.

**All these services support direct withdrawal to Payoneer, which allows authors from countries where Gumroad is not available to earn money.**

I have an article on how to [withdraw money through Payoneer](/monetize/payments/payoneer).

### Buy me a coffee

[Buy me a coffee](https://slobo.click/buymeacoffee) is a service for collecting donations.

The commission on Buy me a coffee is half that of Gumroad – 5%.

1. You can collect donations with an open amount.
2. It's possible to add digital products.
3. There's a built-in recommendation system.

I have an article on [how to sell digital products on Buy Me a Coffee](/monetize/memberships/buy-me-a-coffee).

<Shot src="./66f40eecab0c833d44d47127_65ca2451325d487977326ec8_gumroad-alternative-buy-me-a-coffee.webp" alt="Gumroad alternative: Buy me a coffee" />

### Patreon

[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com) is used for monetizing a community through monthly subscriptions.

Patreon's fees range from 8% to 12%.

1. Patreon has a well-known brand. If your audience is already subscribed to someone on Patreon, it will be easier for them to subscribe to you.
2. You can create different types of subscriptions with various access levels to content.

For more details on [how Patreon works](/monetize/memberships/patreon).

<Shot src="./66f40eecab0c833d44d4712a_65ca26b0ee5183efd95dcbc5_gumroad-alternative-patreon.webp" alt="Gumroad alternative: Patreon" wide />

Both services are expanding their features. Patreon now allows the sale of individual products, and Buy me a coffee has introduced subscription options.

How to choose?

[Buy me a coffee](https://slobo.click/buymeacoffee) is simpler and more straightforward, suitable for those who want to earn through donations and selling individual digital products.

[Patreon](https://www.patreon.com) is better suited for those looking to develop and monetize their community.

### Etsy

[Etsy](https://slobo.click/etsy) is a marketplace for home goods, decor, and art items.

Etsy's fees include a $0.20 listing fee per item and a 6% commission fee.

It's suitable for those creating illustrations or products for artists. You can sell both digital and physical goods.

Note that Etsy does not offer the option to collect donations or monetize a community through subscriptions.

More details on [how to start selling on Etsy](/monetize/marketplaces/etsy).

<Shot src="./66f40eecab0c833d44d4712d_65ca276bd1ee28d616c971c2_gumroad-alternative-etsy.webp" alt="Gumroad alternative: Etsy" wide />

You can use multiple services simultaneously.

For example, sell prints of your illustrations on Etsy, and collect donations or sell your brushes and digital copies of illustrations on Buy me a coffee.

### Conclusions

Selling digital products, collecting donations, and subscriptions from your audience are excellent [monetization tools](/monetize).

Many creators earn passive income, and some even make it their primary job.

When choosing a platform to host your products, ensure that you can withdraw your earnings.
