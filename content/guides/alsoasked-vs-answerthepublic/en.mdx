  - q: Can I use AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic for keyword research in languages other than English?
    a: Yes, both AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic support keyword research in multiple languages. This makes them valuable tools for creating multilingual content and reaching a global audience.
  - q: How often is the data updated on AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic?
    a: AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic rely on real-time data from Google. AlsoAsked scrapes data from Google's People Also Ask section, and AnswerThePublic uses Google's autocomplete suggestions. Thus, the results you see are based on current search trends. However, the frequency with which Google updates its data can influence the freshness of the results.
  - q: Can I use AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic for local SEO?
    a: Yes, both tools can be helpful for local SEO. AlsoAsked allows you to choose a specific region along with the language, enabling you to find hyper-local results. AnswerThePublic helps you identify locally relevant keywords and questions by analyzing Google Autocomplete suggestions, which are often influenced by the searcher's location. By combining the insights from both tools, you can tailor your content to target a specific geographic area.
  - q: Are there any browser extensions for AlsoAsked or AnswerThePublic?
    a: While AlsoAsked doesn't have a dedicated browser extension, AnswerThePublic was acquired by Neil Patel and is available as a part of the Ubersuggest extension. This allows you to perform keyword research and access AnswerThePublic's data directly within your browser while searching on Google. Keep in mind that some features may require a paid Ubersuggest subscription.
  - q: How do AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic compare to the "Searches related to" section at the bottom of Google's results?
    a: AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic offer a more structured and comprehensive approach compared to the "Searches related to" section. AlsoAsked provides a hierarchical view of related questions, showing the connections between them, while "Searches related to" offers a simple list. AnswerThePublic offers broader ideation, going beyond questions to present related prepositions, comparisons, and more in a visual format. In short, these tools provide a deeper and more organized way to understand search intent.

  - q: What is the difference between "questions," "prepositions," and "comparisons" in AnswerThePublic?
    a: AnswerThePublic categorizes its results to provide different perspectives on search intent. "Questions" are queries phrased as questions (who, what, where, when, why, how). "Prepositions" show searches that include prepositions like "for," "with," "to," or "without," revealing relationships between keywords. "Comparisons" display searches that compare things, often using words like "vs," "or," "and." These different categories help you understand the nuances of how people search for information related to your topic.

## Getting to Know AlsoAsked

### What Is AlsoAsked and How Does It Work?

[AlsoAsked](https://slobo.click/alsoasked) is a tool designed to extract questions from Google’s “_People Also Ask_” section.

<Shot src="./67a1f22396cda5e4d5cfb694_google-people-also-ask-questions.webp" alt="Google’s People Also Ask section showing related questions for user-driven content optimization" />

By mining these real user queries, it delivers a wealth of long-tail keyword ideas and uncovers content gaps.

This helps you craft posts, FAQs, or even entire course modules that directly answer what your audience is curious about.

### How AlsoAsked Extracts Google’s People Also Ask

AlsoAsked queries Google and captures the “_People Also Ask_” data in a structured way.

This process lets you:

- **Extract** a hierarchy of related questions
- **Visualize** clusters of similar queries
- **Export** findings in CSV format for deeper analysis

This structured extraction means you can see how one question naturally leads to another—a conversation you can mirror in your content.

### Key Functionalities of AlsoAsked

AlsoAsked offers a range of features designed to streamline your content research:

- **Data Visualization:** Clear visual maps of related questions highlight content opportunities at a glance.
- **Clustering:** Similar questions are grouped together, enabling you to cover topics comprehensively.

<Shot src="./67a1f23ea001742c3e37dcef_alsoasked-keyword-question-cluster.webp" alt="AlsoAsked visual map displaying clusters of related questions for effective keyword research and content planning" wide />

## Understanding AnswerThePublic

### What Is AnswerThePublic and How Does It Work?

AnswerThePublic takes a different approach by using Google autocomplete data to generate question maps and keyword ideas.

It captures common search queries as you type and transforms them into a visual representation of topics and subtopics.

<Shot src="./67a1f206250f8009ce075dd2_google-autocomplete-suggestions-fansly.webp" alt="Google autocomplete results showing search suggestions for the term 'Fansly,' helpful for content idea generation" />

This broader view can spark a range of content ideas beyond the specific queries found in People Also Ask.

### Unique Features of AnswerThePublic

AnswerThePublic offers several distinct features:

- **Visual Question Maps:** Organized layouts of questions and phrases help you see potential topics and subtopics instantly.
- **Keyword Ideation:** In addition to questions, it suggests related keywords to bolster your overall SEO strategy.

## Data Sources and Methodologies: A Comparative Look

### How the Tools Source Their Data

Both tools offer valuable insights, but they use different data sources:

#### AlsoAsked

- Sources data from Google’s People Also Ask
- Focuses on real, user-asked questions for pinpoint content gaps

#### AnswerThePublic

- Uses Google autocomplete data
- Captures a broader variety of queries, resulting in a wider scope of keyword ideas

### Impact of Methodology on Insights

The different data sources influence the insights each tool provides:

- **AlsoAsked** yields highly targeted, long-tail questions that help fill specific content gaps.
- **AnswerThePublic** offers a broader array of ideas, making it ideal for brainstorming and identifying trending topics.

## Implementing Actionable Insights into Your Content Planning

### Step-by-Step Integration Example

Here’s how I integrate these tools into my content workflow:

1. **Identify Core Questions:** Enter a broad topic into AlsoAsked to extract a list of specific user queries.
2. **Visual Brainstorming:** Use AnswerThePublic to create a visual map of related topics, inspiring ideas for blog posts, videos, or course modules.
3. **Cluster and Prioritize:** Group similar questions together. Focus on those that are most relevant to your audience and align with your expertise.
4. **Content Creation:** Structure your content around these clusters. Consider building an FAQ section or dedicating individual posts to the most common questions.
5. **Validate with Analytics:** After publishing, monitor performance using tools like [Google Analytics](/grow/analytics/google-analytics). Adjust your strategy based on the data to continuously improve your content.

Integrating these steps ensures that your content is both data-driven and genuinely helpful to your audience.

## Exploring Alternative Keyword Research Tools

### A Broader Look at the Keyword Landscape

While AlsoAsked and AnswerThePublic are powerful, there are several other tools that might complement your strategy:

### When to Consider These Alternatives

If your content strategy requires both deep query analysis and a broader keyword view, consider integrating these tools.

For example, while AlsoAsked pinpoints specific questions, SEMrush or Ahrefs can provide detailed competitive insights and search volume data for a more rounded approach.
