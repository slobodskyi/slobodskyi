## What is Skrill?

[Skrill](https://slobo.click/skrill) is an international payment system that allows money transfers between countries, receiving payments from websites, and trading cryptocurrency.

Skrill has been operational since 2001 and serves users in 185 countries.

It supports payments in 33 currencies.

### How does Skrill work?

Immediately after registering, you will be able to:

1. **Receive money:** from clients, friends, or relatives.
2. **Withdraw money from websites:** for example, you can [withdraw money from OnlyFans through Skrill](/monetize/memberships/onlyfans).
3. **Make transfers:** to other Skrill users or international money transfers to any account.
4. **Pay for online purchases:** a Skrill account can be linked to various sites and used as payment. The money will be debited from the Skrill balance or a linked bank card.
5. **Trade cryptocurrency:** setting up automatic conditions for transactions is available.

## What are Skrill's fees and tariffs?

Skrill's fees are a bit complicated.

Pay attention to the method of withdrawal.

Withdrawing money to a Mastercard bank card incurs a fee of 4.99%, and to Visa, it's 7.50%.

Fees and services may vary depending on the country.

**Table 1: Main Skrill Fees.**

| Service                             | Cost                |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Account opening                     | Free                |
| Topping up your account from a card | 2.5%                |
| Withdrawing money from Skrill       | from 3.49% to 7.50% |

The percentage depends on the transfer method and the recipient's country.

**Table 2: Fees for transfers and receiving.**

| Service                            | Cost                |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Receiving funds by details         | Free                |
| Receiving funds from a Skrill user | Free                |
| Sending money by bank details      | from 0% to 4.99%    |
| Sending to another Skrill user     | from 2.99% to 3.98% |

If you are not tied to Skrill, consider alternative payment systems.

There are cheaper options regarding fees:

1. **For withdrawing from websites and business operations:** [Payoneer](/monetize/payments/payoneer) (a withdrawal fee of just 2%).
2. **For personal transfers:** [Wise](/monetize/payments/wise) (a fee of up to 2.5%).

## How to Register with Skrill?

Registration with Skrill is free and takes just a few minutes.

### Filling in Account Details

Go to the [Skrill registration page](https://slobo.click/skrill).

Select your preferred language from the top right corner.

Specify:

1. First and last name.
2. Country of citizenship.
3. Desired currency.

If necessary, you can change the currency in the future.

All information must be real and transliterated into Latin characters.

If you have an international passport, copy the details from there.

<Shot src="./678799ef34f9622b1c1b010e_skrill-registratsiya.webp" alt="Skrill Registration Form" />

### Providing Address and Phone Number

Next, you will need to provide your registered address and phone number.

Make sure all the information is correct, as you will need to verify it in the future.

<Shot src="./678799ef34f9622b1c1b0119_skrill-account.webp" alt="How to Register a Skrill Account" />

### Choosing the Purpose of Use

Right after registration, you need to indicate the purpose for which you want to use the payment system.

1. Pay online. If you plan to pay for goods or services on websites using Skrill.
2. Buy and sell cryptocurrency.
3. Money transfer. If you plan to receive money, choose this option as well.

**Whatever you choose, all services will be available to you in the future.**

<Shot src="./678799ef34f9622b1c1b011c_skrill-goal.webp" alt="Purpose of Use When Registering with Skrill" wide />

### Linking a Bank Card

In the last step, you will need to link a bank card.

After linking a card, you will be able to top up your Skrill account balance and withdraw money from the account to the card.

### Verifying Your Account

You can use your Skrill account immediately after registration.

However, depending on the volumes and types of transactions, you may be asked to go through verification after some time.

For verification, you will need:

1. To confirm your identity using a passport (national or international) or a driver's license.
2. To confirm your address. For this, you need to use a document issued within the last 3 months. This can easily be done, for example, by paying for utilities at your registered address through the Monobank app and downloading the receipt. You will have a bank receipt with the required address. The payment amount does not matter. A registration certificate is also acceptable.

To submit documents, you will need to use the Skrill mobile app.

## How to Receive Money in Skrill?

If you want to top up the account yourself, you can use any bank card.

The fee for topping up your account with a card is 2.5%.

If you want a client or acquaintance to top up your Skrill account, they just need to know the email address or phone number associated with your account.

## How to Withdraw Money from Skrill?

There are two ways to withdraw money from your Skrill account:

1. To a bank card.
2. To a Neteller account.

## How to Send Money?

If you need to send money to a client or Skrill user, there are two options:

1. Transfer between Skrill accounts. To make a transfer, you just need to provide the recipient's email or phone number. The money is credited instantly.
2. International money transfer. In any currency to banking details.

When choosing an international money transfer, you will need to specify the recipient's currency, choose where the money will be debited from, and where it will be credited.

Technically, Skrill does not charge a fee for the transfer, but there is a 3.5% commission for currency conversion.

## What are the Alternatives to Skrill?

The choice of a payment system depends on your goals.

Skrill has its advantages: the ability to top up your account with a card and trade cryptocurrency.

If you plan to transfer money to acquaintances in other countries or gamble on gaming sites, Skrill might be suitable.

However, I recommend considering alternatives as well, since Skrill objectively has high fees.

### Skrill vs Payoneer

[Payoneer](https://slobo.click/payoneer) is an international payment system founded in 2005.

Payoneer allows you to:

1. Receive money from partner sites.
2. Receive money directly from clients.
3. Get account details in 8 currencies.

The main disadvantage of Payoneer compared to Skrill is the inability to make personal payments.

Payoneer is used exclusively for business operations.

Fees at Payoneer range from 0% to 3%.

Learn more about [how to create a Payoneer account](/monetize/payments/payoneer).

### Wise vs Skrill

[Wise](https://slobo.click/wise) allows for transfers between countries in different currencies.

Supports both personal transfers and business operations.

Does not charge a currency conversion fee. The transfer fee averages less than 2.5%.

If you need to send money to another country, [read about how Wise works](/monetize/payments/wise).

### Wise vs Payeer

[Payeer](https://slobo.click/payeer) is an electronic wallet for storing fiat and cryptocurrencies.

Payeer does not support transfers to a bank account, but you can withdraw to other payment systems.

Learn more about [receiving and withdrawing money through Payeer](/monetize/payments/payeer).
