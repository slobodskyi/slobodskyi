## What Is a Hashtag?

A hashtag is a word or phrase preceded by the # symbol.

Hashtags simplify content discovery on social media and have become a powerful promotional tool.

For instance, Instagram posts with at least one hashtag get 12.6% more engagement compared to posts without them.

In 2024, the hashtag #TravelTuesday inspired thousands to share their travel adventures, boosting the popularity of travel content and increasing bookings.

**However, how hashtags work changes every year.**

### The Evolution of Hashtags

In 2007, hashtags first appeared on Twitter as a way to organize conversations. They quickly became a tool for joining global discussions, tracking trends, and amplifying content reach.

Following their success on Twitter, hashtags spread to Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn, adapting to each platform’s unique needs:

1. **Instagram:** For discovering new content and improving visibility.
2. **Facebook:** For grouping discussions and events.
3. **LinkedIn:** For professional networking and visibility.

Content creators actively use hashtags to attract audiences, [increase reach](/connect), and spark viral trends.

## How Hashtags Work in 2026

### Instagram: From Quantity to Quality

Hashtags on Instagram remain important, but the focus has shifted to relevance.

- Targeted hashtags work better than generic ones. For example, #WatercolorArtists attracts more attention than #Art.
- Instagram’s algorithm promotes posts with hashtags that match users’ recent interests, such as #EcoFriendlyArt.
- Where Do Hashtags Go on Instagram? Add them to captions or comments to improve readability and avoid visual clutter.

### TikTok: Trends and Communities

On TikTok, hashtags help users discover content and form communities.

- **Communities:** For example, #BookTok united book lovers and influenced bestseller lists.
- **Viral Tags:** Hashtags like #DanceChallenge generate high engagement.
- **What Hashtags to Use on TikTok:** Mix trending and niche hashtags, such as #SmallCreators, to broaden your reach.

### X (formerly Twitter): Real-Time Discussions

Hashtags on X are key to joining live events and trending conversations.

- Use them to search topics and increase tweet visibility.
- Add hashtags strategically to stay at the center of relevant discussions.

### LinkedIn: Building Professional Reputation

LinkedIn relies on precise hashtag use for professional networking.

- **Industry-Specific Tags:** Hashtags like #DigitalMarketing help connect with colleagues.
- **Professional Trends:** Showcase your expertise with relevant hashtags but avoid overloading posts.

Hashtags on social media function like keywords in SEO. They simplify searches and help users find your content.

- Aligning hashtags with your SEO strategy increases visibility.

### Reach on Platforms and Beyond

Hashtags are highly effective within apps but have limited impact on external search engines like Google.

1. **Within Platforms:** Help users find your content faster.
2. **Beyond Platforms:** Use broader [SEO strategies to improve your Google ranking](/connect/organic/seo).
