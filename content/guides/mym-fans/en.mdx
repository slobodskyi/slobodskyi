## What is MYM.fans?

[MYM.fans](https://slobo.click/mym) (Me. You. More) is a platform for content creators, primarily from the adult industry.

It allows models to earn money by offering exclusive content to subscribers for a monthly fee.

<Callout>
  The most popular sites for adult models are [OnlyFans](/monetize/memberships/onlyfans) and [Fansly](/monetize/memberships/fansly). In this article, we will explore the differences between them and how to choose the right site for your work.
</Callout>

### How to Earn on MYM.fans?

Step-by-step:

1. Create and set up your profile.
2. Fill your profile with quality content.
3. Promote your profile through social media or specialized sites.

General Recommendations:

1. **Set a reasonable subscription price:** Study the market and set a competitive price. Start with small amounts and increase as your popularity grows.
2. **Promote your profile:** Use social media to attract subscribers. Post teasers and links to MYM, actively engage with your audience.
3. **Engage with subscribers:** Communicate with subscribers through messages and comments. This will help create a loyal community.
4. **Run promotions and discounts:** Organize sales and offer discounts on subscriptions to attract new and retain current users.

These recommendations work regardless of the chosen site.

### How to Choose a Subscription Price?

Unlike other subscription services, on MYM you choose from four options:

1. €9.99
2. €19.99
3. €49.99
4. €99.99

On OnlyFans and Fansly, you can set the price yourself, from $5 to $500.

<Shot src="./678799f0e2160888ea7a6a4e_7-mym-how-does-it-work.webp" alt="Choosing a subscription price on MYM.fans" />

After creating the account, you need to complete certification.

1. **Send my personal details:** Provide your name, date of birth, and address. This information will not be publicly displayed.
2. **Add a bank account:** Add a bank account for withdrawing money. You can [use Payoneer as an intermediary](/monetize/payments/payoneer) or directly to your account (any bank that supports euro deposits via IBAN will do).
3. **Send my ID card:** Upload a photo of your passport. This is a standard step for all adult platforms to confirm your age.
4. **Confirm my identity:** Upload a selfie holding your passport.
5. **Confirm my mobile phone:** Verify your mobile phone number.
6. **Add 5 posts on my profile:** Add 5 posts. Make sure these 5 posts showcase your content from different angles and interest potential subscribers.
