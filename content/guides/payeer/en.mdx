## What is Payeer?

[Payeer](https://slobo.click/payeer) is an electronic wallet for handling fiat currencies (euros, dollars, rubles) and cryptocurrencies.

The company is registered in Lithuania, has been operational since 2012, and supports transfers in 127 countries.

### What can you do with Payeer?

1. Top up your account. From Visa or Mastercard, for free with cryptocurrency, or through payment systems like Advcash and PerfectMoney.
2. Send money. To another Payeer user, to Advcash, PerfectMoney, or without commission with cryptocurrency.
3. Exchange currencies and cryptocurrencies with each other.
4. Trade cryptocurrencies on the exchange.
5. Integrate Payeer to accept payments on your website. Users will be able to pay with bank cards or cryptocurrency.

### Is it safe to use Payeer?

On [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/payeer.com), Payeer has almost 4,000 positive reviews, with an average rating of 4.8/5. This is a good result, higher than [Payoneer](/monetize/payments/payoneer) or [Skrill](/monetize/payments/skrill).

I have been using Payeer for 3 years, and there have been no problems during this time.

The account allows you to enable two-factor authentication, and notifications are sent to your email and phone with each login.

### What currencies are supported?

Fiat currencies include euros, dollars, and rubles.

Cryptocurrencies include Bitcoin, Tether, Tron, MATIC, Ethereum, Litecoin, DAI, DOT, Bitcoin Cash, BNB, APE, 1INCH, Dash, Dogecoin, UNI, SUSHI, USDC, Ripple, GMT, GRT.

## How to create a Payeer wallet?

You can create an account on [the official website](https://slobo.click/payeer).

Registration occurs in three steps.

1. Enter and confirm your email.
2. Note down the "Secret code" and "Account name."
3. Provide your name and country.

<Shot src="./678799f0532e657011cb143f_payeer-registratsiya.webp" alt="Registration of a Payeer wallet" />

At the second step, Payeer automatically generates a "Secret code" and "Account name," but you can change them to your own.

**In the future, you will log in using the "Account name," so it's important not to lose this information.**

<Shot src="./67879aaeecd00758276e6368_payeer-registratsiya.webp" alt="Security details and Payeer account number" />

### How to verify your account?

Payeer imposes a limit on accounts that have not been verified - a transaction limit of up to $999 per day.

How to increase the limit?

Click on your account number and select the "Verification" section.

<Shot src="./66d23a889e42f7ffe02a281f_65c3c6175e4e66df8dc9fed8_what-is-my-payeer-address.webp" alt="Verification of a Payeer account" />

You will need to:

1. Fill in your personal information.
2. Upload supporting documents.

What documents are needed?

A passport and proof of address.

**Account verification is a standard procedure for payment systems. Information about account opening, transactions, and wallet balance is not shared with third parties.**

## How to add money in Payeer?

After registration, you can see all your balances and you can:

1. Top up your account.
2. Send money.
3. Exchange currencies.
4. Trade on the exchange.

Let's first look at how to top up fiat currencies.

### How to deposit Payeer from a bank card?

To top up your wallet, go to the "Add" section.

Select the currency you want to top up.

Depending on the selected currency, different top-up methods will be available to you.

For example, you can top up a dollar account in the following ways:

1. Payment systems: Advcash, PerfectMoney.
2. Cryptocurrency: USDT.
3. From a card through exchangers: Visa, MasterCard.

<Shot src="./66d23a889e42f7ffe02a281c_65c3ca8f52ddf0d929293c8c_how-to-deposit-money-in-payeer.webp" alt="How to top up Payeer with a bank card or payment systems" />

### How to deposit Payeer through exchangers?

If you need more options to top up or withdraw money from Payeer, you can use exchangers.

To find safe exchangers with reasonable rates, I use the [aggregator Obmify](https://slobo.click/obmify).

How to use the aggregator?

1. Choose what you will be giving: money from a bank card, payment system, or cryptocurrency.
2. Choose what you want to receive: in our case, Payeer.
3. You get a table of exchangers: choose by suitable rate and positive reviews.

Then you go to the exchanger's website.

1. Specify the exchange amount.
2. The wallet or card from which you will be sending money.
3. The details where the funds should be credited.

They give you the details where you need to send the money.

You send it, and within 30 minutes the money is credited to Payeer.

This method can be used to deposit or withdraw money not only to banks but also to other payment systems, which would not be possible otherwise: PayPal, YooMoney, Webmoney.

### How to exchange money within Payeer?

Go to the "Exchange" section.

1. Choose the pair you want to exchange.
2. Enter the amount, and immediately see how much you will receive.
3. Click to exchange.

The exchange happens instantly.

For some pairs, the minimum amount for exchange is $20.

<Shot src="./678799f0532e657011cb1439_65c3cce388960267a626109c_how-to-exchange-money-in-payeer.webp" alt="Exchange of currencies and cryptocurrencies within Payeer" />

### How to trade on the cryptocurrency exchange in Payeer?

The exchange is located in the "Trade" section.

1. Select the cryptocurrency pair you want to work with and place an order to buy or sell.
2. After placing orders, monitor their execution and the market situation. You can cancel an unfulfilled order at any time orplace a new one.
3. After successful trading, you can withdraw the earned funds back to fiat currency or to an external cryptocurrency wallet.
